Professional summary

VAPT practitioner and security researcher focused on authorized web application testing, attack-surface discovery, vulnerability validation, and clean remediation reporting. I prefer manual verification over noisy scanner output: every finding should explain the problem, evidence, impact, affected scope, and fix.

VAPT methodology

01 — Scope

Confirm authorization, test limits, safe windows, contacts, and prohibited actions before any assessment.

02 — Recon

Map subdomains, live hosts, services, endpoints, JavaScript, parameters, screenshots, and public exposures.

03 — Validate

Manually test OWASP classes, access control, auth/session, business logic, APIs, misconfiguration, and impact paths.

04 — Report

Provide evidence, risk, reproduction, CVSS/CWE where useful, remediation guidance, and retest notes.
